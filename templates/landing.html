Statutory Organ Allocation Workflow (State Machine)
End-to-End Traceability
Step 1
Patient Request

Patient submits organ requirement with clinical diagnosis & medical report dossier.

Step 2
Doctor Verification

Physician validates clinical need, reviews lab tests, and assigns priority level (Emergency / High / Normal).

Step 3
Govt Clearance

Statutory authority checks legal compliance and issues official downloadable Approval Certificate.

Step 4
Mortuary / Donor Intake

Mortuary staff flags potential cadaveric donor, verifies consent, and alerts hospital transplant unit.

Step 5
Matching Engine

Automated multi-factor algorithm scores candidates strictly by blood group, priority, and FIFO wait time.

Step 6
Binding & Alert

Organ is bound to top-ranked candidate. Immediate dispatch alerts sent to patient and hospital team.
